# junun_academy
# 🎯 Junun Academy Samastipur

> **Your Dream Government Job Starts Here**

A modern, premium, fully responsive website for **Junun Academy Samastipur**, a Government Job Coaching Institute in Bihar. The website is designed with a military-inspired theme, smooth animations, modern UI/UX, and focuses on providing an engaging experience for students preparing for government recruitment exams.

# 🏛️ About Junun Academy

Junun Academy Samastipur is dedicated to preparing students for various Government Recruitment Examinations through disciplined learning, expert guidance, mock tests, written preparation, and physical training.

### Courses

- Indian Army
- Agniveer
- Bihar Police
- Bihar Daroga (SI)
- SSC GD
- Railway
- Written + Physical Training
